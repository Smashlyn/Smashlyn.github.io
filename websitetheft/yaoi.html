    Location: Inverell, New South Wales
    (Old Armidale Road)

    Event: Yaoi Sighting

    Date: June 31st, 2013

    Time: 4.20pm




    Myself and a mate had finished fossicking for crystals. We were a fair distance away from the vehicle, probably 150m away from the vehicle, we couldn’t see the vehicle. We could smell kind of a sweet smell, like a herbal tea smell and all this. It was real nice. It was around 2 O’Clock – somewhere around in that time period.

    I’ve never smelt that smell before and I thought it might have been a flowering shrub because there are plants in the area, but my mate has done a lot of horticulture and he said that’s definitely not a shrub.

    It was like, you know how you walk into an apothecary and you get that beautiful type smell around it. A bit like that. And there was enticing, arousing kind of smell as well. It was very strong. It was there for around 15 – 20 minutes.

    We didn’t think that much of it, just kept digging until it was time to go home.


    There were a couple of sounds like there was someone near the car, but it’s a bit hard for someone to be out near the car when we’re right out in the bush, you know. I stood up to have a look, but there was no one there. There were a few noises like things being flung through the bush, but I thought it may be a kangaroo or a pig or something.


    But when we actually got back out on the road heading home back towards Inverell, we saw this thing.

    We were driving along, average speed, about 100, and we just saw these guys, you know, just standing on the side of the road. We slowed down to take a better look and we could see them making out in the bush.

    We had a look and they was really really hot, and the face and the chin was too incredible to be Human. I guess they would have been 100 metres away on our left hand side. Right on the edge of the road. It was good daylight.

    I’m 6’4”, they would have been five to six feet tall. At first they weren't looking, and then they looked at the car and just took off. We had a good view of it before they took off. Probably about 15 seconds or so before they took off.

    I weight about 120kg, these guys weighed maybe half, 2/3rds my weight.


    We could see the legs, see the arms, one guys arms came down around the other's waist, the legs were thick and he was pretty tall. He had brownish reddish blacky hair. Darker below. Face was more reddish.


    The hair was long, straight and well kept. Maybe 2 – inches long.

    One of their faces looked over at us - incredible. Like what you’d see on TV, you know. Masculine in the face.

    He was beautiful. He was stunning. He had an incredible jawline – better than mine! So cut, just amazing.

    We slowed down so we could see and we were watching them, then they ran through the bush.

    They ran like a Human but extraordinarily fast. I couldn’t run that fast through the bush without falling over. They was moving.

    The scrub was pretty open, so we could see everything.


    I spoke to my Grandfather because he used to mine out there and he said he’s seen them too. You hear the Myths, but when you see it, its like whoa.


    I can’t believe I’ve actually seen some.
